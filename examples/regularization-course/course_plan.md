## Lectures

### Lecture 1 — Regularization Foundations

**Topics:**
- Bias-variance tradeoff and overfitting
- L2 (Ridge) penalty
- L1 (Lasso) penalty
- Geometric intuition: constraint regions and sparsity
- Bayesian / MAP interpretation of penalties

**Estimated time:** 90 min
**Prerequisites within course:** none
**Announce-only sections:** elastic net (covered in Lecture 2)

### Lecture 2 — Regularization in Practice & Deep Learning

**Topics:**
- Elastic net
- Early stopping
- Dropout
- Data augmentation
- Batch normalization as implicit regularization
- Cross-validation for tuning regularization strength

**Estimated time:** 90 min
**Prerequisites within course:** Lecture 1
**Announce-only sections:** none

## Labs

### Lab 1 — Comparing Regularization Methods
*(managed by `/course-maker lab` pipeline — see `labs/lab1/`)*

Implement and compare Ridge/Lasso/Elastic Net regression and dropout in a
small neural net; tune regularization strength via cross-validation.
